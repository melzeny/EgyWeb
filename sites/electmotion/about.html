Importing and supplying cable and components for Egypt's infrastructure

Electro Motion is an Egyptian company that sources cables and electrical components from international manufacturers and supplies them to contractors, utilities and railway projects, matched to each project's technical specification.

What we do

A specification-first approach to cable supply

Importing cable and components for infrastructure and railway projects means matching international products to Egyptian project requirements, and managing the logistics in between. Our approach covers four areas.

01

Specification-first sourcing

Every enquiry starts with the technical requirement. We review drawings, BOQs and cable schedules and match products against the IEC, EN or client standard the project calls for.

02

Import and customs handled

We manage shipping, customs clearance and import documentation in Egypt, so material arrives on site ready to install without delays at the border.

03

Documentation for approval

Factory test reports, certificates of origin and conformity documents are prepared and organised for consultant and client submittal review.

04

Engineering support

Our team helps with cable selection, sizing questions and technical submittals during tendering and through to execution on site.

Our values

What we hold ourselves to

These principles guide how we work with contractors, consultants and operators on every enquiry.

Technical accuracy

We quote against the actual specification, not a generic catalogue, so what arrives on site is what your project needs.

Reliability

Clear lead times and delivery planned around your site programme, communicated honestly from quotation to handover.

Responsiveness

Tenders move quickly. We aim to turn around technical and commercial offers promptly so you can meet your submission dates.

Long-term partnership

We aim to be a supplier you can return to project after project, not a one-off transaction.

Electro Motion sources products against IEC, EN and ITU specifications as required by each project. These are specifications we source against, not certifications held by Electro Motion.
