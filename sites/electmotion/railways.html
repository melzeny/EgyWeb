Cable and component solutions for railway and metro projects

From trackside signalling to traction power and overhead line equipment, we supply cable and components that meet the technical demands of railway, metro and light rail projects, sourced to your project specification.

Typical scope

Built for the full railway cable schedule

Railway and metro projects call for cable and components across five demanding areas of the line, from the depot to the tunnel.

01

Signalling & control cables

Signalling, axle-counter, track-circuit and balise cables for interlocking and train detection systems. See railway & signalling cables.

02

Traction power cabling

Medium- and low-voltage power cables feeding traction substations and trackside power supply. See MV & HV power cables.

03

Overhead line equipment

Contact and messenger wire, droppers, clamps and insulators for overhead contact systems on electrified lines. See overhead line equipment.

04

Rolling-stock wiring

Thin-wall and standard-wall cables for on-board wiring, screened and armoured where electromagnetic compatibility is required.

05

Tunnel fire performance (LSZH)

Halogen-free, low-smoke cable constructions for tunnel and underground sections, referenced against IEC 60332, IEC 61034 and IEC 60754.
