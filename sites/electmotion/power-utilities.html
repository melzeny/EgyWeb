Cable and component solutions for power distribution networks and substations

From MV and HV feeders to switchgear panels and earthing systems, we supply cable and components that meet the technical demands of distribution networks, substations and utility infrastructure, sourced to your project specification.

Typical scope

Built for the full distribution cable schedule

Power and utility projects call for cable and components from the incoming feeder to the final earth connection, across four demanding areas.

01

MV & HV power cables

XLPE-insulated medium- and high-voltage cables for distribution feeders and substation interconnections. See MV & HV power cables.

02

LV power & control cables

Low-voltage power and control cables for substation auxiliaries, protection and station services. See LV power & control cables.

03

Joints & terminations

Heat-shrink, cold-shrink and pre-moulded joints and terminations to connect and terminate cables safely. See cable joints & terminations.

04

Switchgear & distribution components

Protection, distribution and metering components for LV and MV panels and substation power systems. See switchgear & distribution components.

05

Earthing & lightning protection

Earthing rods, conductors and bonding materials to protect substation equipment, personnel and structures.
